### How to Run & Sync the CareGrid Dashboard with Your Notebook

1. **Test Immediately in Preview:**
   * Click the **Preview** button in the top-right corner of the code editor.
   * Click **Randomize Patient Vitals** in the top navigation bar (with `Randomize Any Profile (profile=None)` or a specific tier selected) to execute your exact Python `simulate_random_patient()` and `CareGridBehavioralVitalTriageEngine` logic directly in the browser.
2. **Test the 3-Tier Real-Time Alert System & Audio Alarms:**
   * **Tier 1 (Early Warning / Minor Indicators):** Displays a clean, non-intrusive blue notification banner at the top of the dashboard stating the exact Tier 1 trigger reason and active symptoms with **no audio alarm**.
   * **Tier 2 (Moderate / Progressive Indicators):** Launches a centered amber pop-up modal accompanied by a repeating clinical beep alarm (synthesized via the browser's Web Audio API) until you click **Acknowledge Alert & Silence Beep**.
   * **Tier 3 (Critical / Emergency Indicators):** Launches a full-screen, pulsing crimson **Code Red Emergency** overlay with a loud, repeating two-tone ICU siren, displaying the probable medical issue/diagnosis, ongoing emergency symptoms, critical vital sign breaches, and a **Dispatch Emergency Response & Acknowledge** button.
3. **Sync Directly with Your Jupyter Notebook (`Cell 65`):**
   * If you want the web dashboard to display the exact patient randomized by Python at the end of `Cell 65`, add these 3 lines to the very bottom of `Cell 65` in your notebook:
     ```python
     import json
     with open('patient_live_state.json', 'w') as f:
         json.dump({'vitals': random_patient_vitals, 'triage': random_patient_tier}, f, default=str, indent=2)
     ```
   * Then click **Sync Python JSON** in the top-right corner of the CareGrid header to fetch or upload `patient_live_state.json`.
